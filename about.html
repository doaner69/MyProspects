Why we exist

Demand should not disappear because a form stayed empty.

Marketing creates attention across dozens of touchpoints, but most systems only recognize the small fraction of people who self-identify. MyProspects connects those fragmented signals into a usable identity and gives teams a clear path to action.

  • Resolve. Connect approved signals to persistent person-level profiles.
  • Understand. Add fit, journey, and intent context.
  • Activate. Deliver audiences to the channels already in use.
