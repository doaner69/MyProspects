Four operating models

From unknown activity to a measurable next step.

Each playbook starts with the signal, adds decision context, and defines a responsible action.

B2B pipeline

Target-account visitor routing

Resolve target-account traffic, rank buying-group activity, and route a concise context package to the account owner.

  • Signal: repeat visits to solution and pricing pages
  • Decision: company fit plus topic intensity
  • Action: CRM task and coordinated nurture audience

B2C growth

High-value browse recovery

Recognize strong product or service research and place the person into a relevant channel sequence.

  • Signal: high-value category and comparison activity
  • Decision: engagement depth and recency
  • Action: personalized media and lifecycle follow-up

Recruitment

Passive candidate discovery

Identify sustained interest in role families and give recruiters a timely, context-rich outreach prompt.

  • Signal: repeated careers and role-page engagement
  • Decision: role fit and visit frequency
  • Action: recruiter review and talent community routing

Nonprofit

Cause-interest activation

Recognize meaningful program research and create a carefully governed donor or volunteer engagement path.

  • Signal: cause, impact, and event research
  • Decision: campaign relevance and consent controls
  • Action: segmented outreach and location-aware activation

How to use a playbook

Define success before adding another signal.

Start with a narrow audience and one clear action. Measure signal quality, routing speed, audience acceptance, and downstream progression before increasing scope.
