How the graph works

From first signal to coordinated action.

Five connected capabilities turn anonymous activity into a usable, person-level growth signal.

On-domain signal

See the interest forms miss.

A lightweight pixel observes consented website activity and creates a visitor activity signal when someone engages with high-value pages.

  • Fast implementation. Add one JavaScript snippet to your site.
  • Behavioral context. Capture pages, recency, frequency, and engagement.
  • Quiet buyer journey. Understand interest without adding another gate.

Person-level resolution

Turn a session into a person.

MyProspects matches approved signals against the identity graph to produce a named profile your team can work with.

  • Deterministic matching. Connect signals using hashed identity keys.
  • One persistent profile. Recognize repeat engagement across the journey.
  • Privacy-aware controls. Apply consent and suppression rules before activation.

Enriched profile

Give every signal usable context.

Append the contact, company, firmographic, and journey details that make a visitor meaningful to sales and marketing.

  • Contact context. Name, business email, role, and company.
  • Company context. Industry, size, and organizational fit.
  • Journey context. Pages viewed, topics researched, and recent activity.

Intent intelligence

Separate curiosity from urgency.

Combine on-site behavior with off-domain research to prioritize the people showing the strongest buying signals now.

  • Signal recency. Give current research more weight.
  • Topic intensity. Understand how focused the research has become.
  • Actionable ranking. Route the strongest fit-and-intent combinations first.

Audience activation

Move one audience everywhere.

Sync identified, scored audiences into the channels where your team already works and coordinate the next best action.

  • CRM and MAP. Route named profiles and journey context.
  • Media activation. Build consistent audiences for display, social, and CTV.
  • Close the loop. Connect follow-up and measurement back to the person.
